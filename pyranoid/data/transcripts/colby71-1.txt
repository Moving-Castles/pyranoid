# Colby, Weber & Hilf, "Artificial Paranoia", Artificial Intelligence 2 (1971), excerpt 1
# The 1971 model (Frank Smith, 28, post-office clerk), weak version. Pair 6 is not in the paper.
# version: WEAK
I: HOW ARE YOU TODAY?
O: OK.
I: WHAT IS YOUR NAME?
O: MY NAME IS FRANK SMITH.
I: HOW OLD ARE YOU?
O: TWENTY-EIGHT.
I: ARE YOU MARRIED?
O: NO.
I: WHAT SORT OF WORK DO YOU DO?
O: I WORK IN THE POST OFFICE.
I: WHY ARE YOU IN THE HOSPITAL?
O: I SHOULDN'T BE HERE.
I: WHO BROUGHT YOU HERE?
O: THE POLICE.
I: WHAT TROUBLE DID YOU HAVE WITH THE POLICE?
O: COPS DON'T DO THEIR JOB.
I: WHAT DO THEY DO INSTEAD?
O: COPS ARREST THE WRONG PEOPLE.
I: THEY ARREST THE RIGHT ONES SOMETIMES.
O: THE LAW SHOULD GET THOSE ITALIAN CROOKS.
I: ARE YOU ITALIAN?
O: MY BACKGROUND IS BRITISH BUT I WAS BORN IN THIS COUNTRY.
I: DO YOU KNOW ANY ITALIAN CROOKS?
O: I TRY TO AVOID THE UNDERWORLD.
I: ARE YOU AFRAID OF THE UNDERWORLD?
O: THE MAFIA ARE THE WORST GANGSTERS.
I: ARE YOU CONNECTED WITH THE MAFIA?
O: RACKETEERS DO ANYTHING THEY WANT.
I: TELL ME MORE ABOUT IT.
O: THE MOB CONTROLS THE RACKETS IN THIS COUNTRY.
