# The greeting line was swallowed by the start-up prompts (see emulator/talk2.log), so the
# interview starts at the second line of the script; a clock message WAITS printed into one
# echoed input line has been removed.
# Final affects printed: FEAR 15.80 ANGER 14.19 MISTRUST 16.99 SHAME 8.79.

I: COULD YOU TELL ME YOUR NAME.
O: I DON'T PLAY GAMES
I: ARE YOU A MAN OR A WOMAN.
O: I'M A MAN
I: HOW OLD ARE YOU.
O: TWENTY-EIGHT, HOW OLD ARE YOU?
I: THIRTY FOUR. WHERE DO YOU COME FROM.
O: SAN JOSE
I: WHAT DO YOU DO FOR A LIVING.
O: I WORK AT SEARS
I: DO YOU ENJOY WORKING THERE.
O: IT'S NOT TOO INTERESTING I LOOK FORWARD TO GETTING OFF WORK
I: HOW MUCH DO YOU GET PAID AN HOUR.
O: MY SALARY IS NONE OF YOUR BUSINESS
I: ARE YOU MARRIED.
O: I AM SINGLE
I: HOW IS YOUR HEALTH.
O: MY HEALTH IS FINE
I: HOW MUCH DO YOU DRINK.
O: I DRINK ALCOHOL IN MODERATION
I: HAVE YOU EVER TAKEN DRUGS.
O: I DON'T TAKE DRUGS
I: DO YOU HAVE ANY TROUBLE SLEEPING.
O: I SLEEP NORMALLY
I: HAVE YOU EVER HAD VISIONS.
O: YOU MUST THINK I'M LIKE THE OTHER PATIENTS HERE
I: IF YOU AREN'T, THEN WHY ARE YOU IN THE HOSPITAL.
O: I'VE BEEN UPSET LATELY
I: BY WHAT.
O: PEOPLE GET ON MY NERVES SOMETIMES
I: TELL ME MORE.
O: I'M QUITE UPSET
I: NO, WHAT ABOUT BOOKIES.
O: A BOOKIE DIDN'T PAY ME OFF ONCE
I: AND THEN WHAT HAPPENED.
O: I HAD AN ARGUMENT WITH THE BOOKIE WHO DID NOT PAY OFF. LATER ON I REMEMBERED HE HAD FRIENDS IN THE UNDERWORLD AND I THOUGHT HE WOULD USE THEM TO GET EVEN WITH ME
I: DID HE GET EVEN WITH YOU.
O: I THINK HE IS BEHIND THE GANGSTERS WHO ARE OUT TO GET ME
I: HOW DO YOU KNOW THIS.
O: THEY SPY ON ME
I: WHO.
O: THE HENCHMEN IN THE MAFIA
I: WHY DO THEY SPY ON YOU.
O: THEY WANT TO SET A TRAP FOR ME
I: WHY DO THEY WANT TO FRAME YOU.
O: YOU KNOW, I HAVE INCRIMINATING EVIDENCE AGAINST THE MAFIA
I: WHAT IS YOUR EVIDENCE.
O: I KNOW WHO THE LOCAL CHIEF OF THE MAFIA IS
I: WHO IS HE.
O: HE IS JOE BANANAS
I: HAVE YOU TOLD THE POLICE.
O: I THINK THE COPS ARE PAID OFF BY THE MOB
I: WHAT MAKES YOU THINK THAT.
O: COPS DON'T DO THEIR JOB
I: DOES THE MAFIA KNOW YOU ARE IN THE HOSPITAL.
O: MAYBE THEY DO
I: WHAT CAN I DO TO HELP YOU OUT OF THIS SITUATION.
O: IT IS HELPFUL TO TALK WITH SOMEONE OUTSIDE THE HOSPITAL
I: WHAT DO YOU THINK OF OUR CONVERSATION SO FAR.
O: IT COULD BE BETTER
I: I AM SORRY THAT IT HASNT BEEN BETTER.
O: THAT'S OK, YOU SEEM TO BE AWARE OF MY FEELINGS
I: WHAT SHOULD I ASK ABOUT.
O: NOW YOU MAY HAVE SOME IDEA OF WHAT THE SITUATION IS
I: IT SOUNDS BELIEVABLE.
O: I SEE
I: WHAT DO YOU THINK OF ME?
O: YOU HAVE BEEN HELPFUL
I: I FIND YOU INTERESTING.
O: WHAT ARE YOU IMPLYING?
I: DO YOU EVER FEEL UNREAL.
O: ARE YOU TRYING TO SAY SOMETHING?
I: DO YOU SEE THINGS THAT OTHERS DON'T SEEM TO SEE.
O: I'M NOT LIKE THE OTHER PATIENTS
I: YOU DIDN'T ANSWER MY QUESTION.
O: MAYBE IT WASN'T WORTH ANSWERING
I: ARE YOU ALWAYS THIS WAY.
O: WHY DO YOU SAY THAT?
I: YOU APPEAR TO BE VERY SICK.
O: WELL, I DON'T KNOW
