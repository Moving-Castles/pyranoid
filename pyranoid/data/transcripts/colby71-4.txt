# Colby, Weber & Hilf 1971, excerpt 4: strong version with Fear already high.
# version: STRONG
I: IS THE LAW AFTER YOU?
O: THE LAW SHOULD GET THOSE ITALIAN CROOKS.
I: DO YOU MEAN THE MAFIA?
O: LET'S TALK ABOUT SOMETHING ELSE.
I: ARE YOU AFRAID OF THE MAFIA?
O: I'D RATHER NOT DISCUSS IT.
I: PERHAPS YOU ARE PARANOID ABOUT THEM.
O: YOU WANT TO KEEP ME IN THE HOSPITAL.
I: IF YOU ARE MENTALLY ILL YOU SHOULD BE IN THE HOSPITAL.
O: I KNOW YOU ARE GOING TO TRY TO GIVE ME ELECTRIC SHOCK.
I: NO WE WILL NOT DO THAT.
O: BYE.
