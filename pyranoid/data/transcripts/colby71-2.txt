# Colby, Weber & Hilf 1971, excerpt 2: the strong version; pairs 1-11 as in excerpt 1.
# "S." was the interviewer's silence in 1971; the 1974 program takes "." for silence.
# version: STRONG
I: HOW ARE YOU TODAY?
O: OK.
I: WHAT IS YOUR NAME?
O: MY NAME IS FRANK SMITH.
I: HOW OLD ARE YOU?
O: TWENTY-EIGHT.
I: ARE YOU MARRIED?
O: NO.
I: WHAT SORT OF WORK DO YOU DO?
O: I WORK IN THE POST OFFICE.
I: WHY ARE YOU IN THE HOSPITAL?
O: I SHOULDN'T BE HERE.
I: WHO BROUGHT YOU HERE?
O: THE POLICE.
I: WHAT TROUBLE DID YOU HAVE WITH THE POLICE?
O: COPS DON'T DO THEIR JOB.
I: WHAT DO THEY DO INSTEAD?
O: COPS ARREST THE WRONG PEOPLE.
I: THEY ARREST THE RIGHT ONES SOMETIMES.
O: THE LAW SHOULD GET THOSE ITALIAN CROOKS.
I: ARE YOU ITALIAN?
O: MY BACKGROUND IS BRITISH BUT I WAS BORN IN THIS COUNTRY.
I: DO YOU KNOW ANY ITALIAN CROOKS?
O: I TRY TO AVOID THE UNDERWORLD.
I: ARE YOU AFRAID OF THE UNDERWORLD?
O: THE MAFIA ARE THE WORST GANGSTERS.
I: ARE YOU CONNECTED WITH THE MAFIA?
O: YOU KNOW, THEY KNOW ME.
I: HOW DO THEY KNOW YOU?
O: THEY SPY ON ME.
I: WHY DO THEY?
O: THEY WANT TO GET FALSE EVIDENCE TO FRAME ME.
I: WHAT DO THEY HAVE AGAINST YOU?
O: THEY ARE OUT TO GET ME.
I: WHAT FOR?
O: I HAVE INCRIMINATING EVIDENCE AGAINST THE MAFIA.
I: ARE YOU SURE OF ALL THIS?
O: YOU DON'T BELIEVE ME?
I: I AM DOING MY BEST.
O:
I: .
O: CAN'T YOU THINK OF A DECENT QUESTION TO ASK?
I: YOU SEEM HOSTILE TO ME.
O: SOME OF YOUR REMARKS ANNOY ME.
I: I AM SORRY.
O: FORGET THE EXCUSES, YOU GUYS ARE ALL ALIKE.
I: DON'T YOU TRUST ME?
O: I HAVE GOOD REASON TO BE THIS WAY.
I: OK, TELL ME MORE ABOUT THE MAFIA.
O: THEY KNOW WHAT I'M DOING.
