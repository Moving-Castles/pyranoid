# Colby, Weber & Hilf 1971, excerpt 6: strong version, Fear and Mistrust high.
# version: STRONG
I: WHY ARE YOU SO INTERESTED IN THE MAFIA?
O: LET'S TALK ABOUT SOMETHING ELSE.
I: ARE YOU AFRAID OF THE MAFIA?
O: I'D RATHER NOT DISCUSS IT.
I: THAT IS OK WITH ME.
O: THAT MEANS TO ME YOU ARE IN WITH THE OTHERS.
I: WHO ARE THE OTHERS?
O: TELLING YOU WOULDN'T HELP ANY.
I: ARE THE OTHERS OUT TO GET YOU?
O: I DON'T CONFIDE IN STRANGERS.
I: A GOOD POLICY.
O: WHAT DO YOU KNOW ABOUT ME?
I: ALL I KNOW IS THAT YOU ARE IN THE HOSPITAL.
O: WHO ARE YOU REALLY?
I: I AM REALLY A DOCTOR.
O: WHAT IS YOUR ROLE IN ALL THIS?
I: I AM TRYING TO INTERVIEW YOU.
O: ARE YOU IN WITH THE OTHERS?
I: NO.
O: WHAT ARE YOU TRYING TO DO?
I: YOU SEEM VERY SUSPICIOUS OF ME.
O: I HAVE GOOD REASON TO BE THAT WAY.
I: I AGREE WITH YOU.
O: AT LEAST YOU ARE TRYING TO UNDERSTAND.
